# 06 «فسحة الهدوء» The Quiet Gap (revision 2)

**Idea.** A voice-note waveform that fades out, rests, and fades back in. A small Kairouan prayer rug stands in the rest: sound all day, a silent slot at prayer time.

**How silence reads.** On each side the sound dies down step by step: a tall bar, a short bar, a round dot, a smaller fainter dot, then nothing, as WhatsApp draws silence. The quiet stretch is 11.6dp per side; colours fade with the sound. No speaker, no slash.

**How prayer reads.** A madder rug (15 x 22dp, 2dp corners) with a thin cream frame, one Kairouan lozenge (ring and core) and a soft fringe of 6 rounded tassels at each end. No niche or arch, so no door, keyhole or mihrab.

**Palette.** Sand `#F6EFE3`. Slate bars fading toward the rug: `#46658D`, `#7C93B2`, then dots `#9DAEC4` and `#B9C5D4`. Rug and fringe `#B5412F`; frame and lozenge `#F6EFE3`. The rug is the only saturated colour.

**Small sizes and themed icon.** At 48px the fade, the dots and a framed red rug read; the lozenge becomes a cream speck. At 24px it is a red tile between bars. Themed: one colour, frame and lozenge cut out.

**What changed.** Fading dots make the silence visible, the envelope decays into the rug, and the arch became the lozenge. The rug is no taller than the loudest bar (28dp with fringe), and the heights differ left and right, like recorded speech. Its body is 79% of that bar, not 60%, so the rug survives 48px. I did not use a 44dp rug: it would become the peak again.

**Risks.** Tall ends and a small middle can read as a dumbbell. A waveform still says "audio" first. Ink is light (15.5%) because the safe circle caps the width.

H01 · Implemented · Presentation refined 26 October 2026

Full source details are part of الشرح

Refined on 26 October. The single الشرح control reveals the narration and explanation followed by المصدر وعدد التكرار: complete references, grading notes, links, reminder/default counts and editorial information. The separate source chip and modal sheet have been removed. Entries without narration or explanation still have access. Short home-entry and market qualifications remain beside the reading. The detail card handles its own taps and accessibility grouping; source links keep separate button actions. AdhkarReader.kt:580.

Original finding: The reader cannot open its full source notes

The reader initializes sources to false and passes the only sources = true assignment as onSources to DhikrPage. DhikrPage declares that callback but never calls it, and the menu has no source action. Its visible reference uses dhikrCitations(), which removes grades, narrators and editorial qualifications. The full source sheet exists but has no reachable opening action.

Observed example

home_enter_bismillah stores: سنن أبي داود 5096؛ صححه الألباني ثم رجع إلى تضعيفه للانقطاع. The reading page reduces this to سنن أبي داود 5096; the explanation does not repeat the weakening. For market, the explanation does retain a weakness warning, so this review does not claim all its caution is missing.

Why it matters

Readers lose the app's own qualifications about disputed reports and repetition counts, even though those notes are present in the catalog. This is a source-transparency defect; it does not establish that the underlying narration is fabricated.

Recommended change

Connect an accessible source action to onSources and keep the full reference available. For weak or disputed entries, show a short status beside the narration as well, while retaining the attributed assessments in the full source notes.

H02 · Implemented · 25 October 2026

Precise source references and attribution notes are available

Implemented. Added precise references and source links for both fitrah occasions, the Fatima supplication and both non-Bukhari Ayat al-Kursi routes. The newborn wording is accurately labeled a general congratulatory supplication, with its related sources and attribution limits explained. Three death-remembrance entries now separate narration sources from grading evidence. adhkar-sources.md:26.

Original finding: Make the incomplete references independently traceable

Several entries name a large collection, narrator, or scholar without enough detail to locate the exact wording or grading. The existing source document repeats some of these general references. A book title alone does not let a reader distinguish different versions, occasions, or scholarly assessments.

Why it matters

These gaps prevented complete verification of some source branches despite the otherwise extensive catalog documentation. They are not evidence that the narrations are fabricated or that the named scholar disagrees.

Recommended change

Record collection + edition/number or volume/page + stable URL, and separately record grader + grading work/number. Keep distinct references for distinct occasions and wording variants. Preserve existing authentic variants and explicitly attributed differences of grading.

  • Ayat al-Kursi: the morning/evening al-Hakim report, after-prayer al-Nasa’i report, and al-Albani grading need precise locators. The after-prayer route is corroborated at al-Sunan al-Kubra 9848 (9/44), as cited by HadeethEnc 10950. The exact morning/evening al-Hakim route remains unresolved in this review.
  • fitrah_islam / fitrah_islam_evening: «مسند أحمد (حديث عبد الرحمن بن أبزى)» needs a report number or edition/page and a source for each occasion. DhikrAdditionalCatalog.kt:569.
  • ya_hayyu: the al-Nasa’i/al-Hakim collections and the attributed al-Albani grading need their own precise citations. DhikrAdditionalCatalog.kt:106.
  • newborn: the app correctly calls this an athar, not a Prophetic hadith, but the attribution to al-Hasan al-Basri has no book or locator. Supply the original athar source and its transmission assessment, or label the wording as a general congratulatory supplication until that attribution is documented. DhikrAdditionalCatalog.kt:478.
  • Named scholarly grades: retain a stable source for the named grader, not only the original hadith collection. The three non-Sahihayn death entries have compatible al-Albani metadata in the mirror, but their app links do not directly establish that named assessment; the existing document does give some grading-work references. This is a provenance improvement, not evidence of a wrong grade.

Separate questions retained from the review

The confirmed source-access and locator defects above are fixed. The following items were not confirmed errors, so their wording was not changed speculatively.

  • Tirmidhi 3432 (afflicted): the exact al-Albani grade wording still requires a work/edition comparison; the app says Hasan and the secondary index says Sahih.
  • Sleep-qini: the precise qualification excluding three repetitions was not reverified. Bika: the selected al-Adab al-Mufrad 1199 wording still needs direct checking. Hamm_quran: the wording and Ahmad attribution have scholarly-site corroboration, but number 3712 and its precise named grading remain unverified.
  • Ibn Majah 4171 — evidence distinction preserved: the new primary links to al-Silsilah al-Sahihah 401 explain the chain and supporting reports. The literal Hasan label is sourced to a separately identified secondary index; the source panel states this limit rather than implying the primary pages print that label.

What the evidence supports

  • Death and closing cards: all 16 hadith records were checked. Seven match HadeethEnc directly; seven combine its corroboration with exact mirrored wording; Ibn Majah 4171 and 4259 have mirror-only corroboration. The mirror attributes Hasan to al-Albani for both, and Hasan Sahih for Tirmidhi 2307. No contrary grading conclusion was established.
  • Salawat: all four additional wordings match their cited narrations after punctuation/diacritic normalization in the mirror. Differences such as «وأزواجه» versus «وعلى أزواجه» belong to documented narration variants and should be preserved.
  • Fasting: the seven explanations are supported summaries. Bukhari 1988 and the alternative placement 1661 are not conflicting references. The Monday passage in Muslim 1162 was corroborated through the mirror, with its Arabic numbering checked separately from the mirror’s sequential ID.
  • Editorial distinctions remain explicit: combined formulas and marked excerpts retain their wording. The newborn wording is now labeled a general supplication; it is not presented as a verbatim athar. This resolves the attribution gap without changing the displayed prayer.
  • Keep source limits visible: count 1 can be a reading default, and reminder goals/times are personal settings. The morning placement of the evening-refuge formula is documented as an editorial choice; Muslim’s cited evening report does not itself establish a morning-specific prescription. Inclusion of al-Ikhlas under the post-prayer «المعوذات» wording involves interpretation, discussed by HadeethEnc 65103; it was not treated as a proven miscitation.

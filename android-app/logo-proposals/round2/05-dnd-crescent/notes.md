# 05 «هلال السكينة» Quiet Crescent

**Idea.** The phone's Do Not Disturb crescent shelters a prayer rug: the quiet mode protects the prayer.

**How silence reads.** A white crescent on a solid Samsung / Sidi Bou Said blue tile, opening to the upper right: the look of the DND Quick Settings tile when it is switched on (One UI, HyperOS, ColorOS, iOS). Nothing is slashed or crossed.

**How prayer reads.** An upright cream rug with a madder mihrab niche (pointed arch), a small lozenge and tassel bands at both ends. The niche turns "a card" into "a prayer rug". No figure, no star.

**Palette.** Tile #3D86D2 to #1D5FA6, crescent #FFFFFF, rug and fringe #F6E9CF, niche #B5412F.

**Small sizes and themed icon.** At 48px: crescent plus a cream rug with a red arch. At 24px: the crescent and a light tile with a red spot. Themed layer: rug with the niche cut out and the lozenge filled again, fringe bands split off by a 0.8dp gap; one layer cannot go solid below 36px, so the cut-out is large instead.

**What changed.** Rug 13.6 x 22 to 15.6 x 24.6dp, centred on the hollow with 3.2dp clearance; crescent thinned (thickest part 16.6 to 13.4dp) and the mark recentred (ink centroid 54.3, 54.0). Thin tassels became solid tassel bands. Blue beat daylight after the fix (edge on light wallpapers, reads "quiet mode on"); daylight kept as `_var/day` with the deeper #B9D3EE ground. The critics' "keep the exact DND thickness" was not followed: the larger rug would not fit.

**Variants.** `blue-lozenge` (a playing card), `blue-red` (framed madder field), `blue-attached` (battlements), `r1-*` (previous round).

**Risks.** The crescent reads as the hilal first for many; white on blue can suggest sleep. Pixel's DND glyph is not a moon. Crescent plus object is a common prayer-app formula.

# 02 · «طمأنينة» Shh

**Idea.** A finger held across closed lips, the "shh" gesture. Drawn as one white figure, it is also the letter ط, which starts the word طمأنينة (stillness in prayer).

**Silence.** Everyone knows this gesture, and nothing is crossed out. The finger stands in front of the lips, with a thin blue gap on each side where it crosses them. It has a rounded tip, a nail, a slight taper, and it ends just below the lower lip. The lips have a cupid's bow dip, a fuller lower lip, and a thin mouth line that tapers into the corners.

**Prayer.** The finger sits left of centre and the lips run on to the right, the way the stem of ط stands at the left end of its loop. The mouth line is the loop's counter. Sidi Bou Said door blue makes it feel Tunisian. There is no sacred symbol in the icon, so nothing religious is ever silenced.

**Palette.** Gradient #2169B5 to #0F4C8F, artwork #FFFFFF.

**Small sizes.** At 48 px you can still see the finger, the nail, both gaps, the bow and the mouth line. At 24 px the gaps and the nail merge, but the outline of a finger across lips holds up. The themed icon has the same outline, with the gaps, nail and mouth line cut as real holes.

**Variants (`_var/`).**
- `a-offset`: a long, thin finger with a round end. It read as a pill or a lipstick.
- `k-centred`: the finger in the exact centre. It is the strongest "shh", but it hides the bow and looks like a candle in a dish.
- `s-final-moon`: adds a crescent. The crescent plus "shh" also says "bedtime", and it shrinks to a speck at 24 px.

The final keeps one clean idea.

**Risks.** Prayer shows only through the ط and the colour. Some viewers may see a lipstick or a cosmetics app. Lips may feel too personal for a prayer app.

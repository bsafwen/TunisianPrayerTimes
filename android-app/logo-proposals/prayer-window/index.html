02 / THE THINKING

Portico Rhythm

The tower and flat prayer-hall roof meet directly. A single continuous contour travels around the minaret, hall, and entrance. The warm balcony band attaches to both sides of the minaret shaft. The doorway is centered beneath its own broad hall.

Tradeoff: Its identity comes from the square minaret and Islamic doorway rather than a dome, making it less literal than a conventional mosque pictogram.

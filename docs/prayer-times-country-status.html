Prayer Times by Country

Search a country to see how the app computes its six times today: the default method, the authority behind it, the zones, how close the app comes to the official table on days it was not fitted on, the label the app shows, and what is still pending.

Release builds open only Tunisia today. Every other country here works in development builds and opens in a release build once the region switch (REGIONS_OPEN_IN_RELEASE) is turned on for the release that ships both regions.

Since 17 October the perpetual tables (Qatar, Kuwait, Libya, Iraq, Lebanon, Palestine, Mauritania) are shown as printed, at the year each was computed for, and tier A can be reached in any country whose zone passes the gate. Since 20 October the app's chip is «رسمية» at an authority's zone in its own country and for a national body's own method, «فلكية» everywhere else; the tiers below are the evidence behind it. Since 18 October Syria follows its ministry's mosque screens, region by region.

“Held out” below means scored on days the fit never saw; “exact” is the same minute as the official table.

02 / THE THINKING

Quiet Joy Mihrab

The jade curve keeps its welcoming upward movement beneath a generous pointed mihrab. Two small openings separate the architecture from the soft embrace, keeping the interior light and spacious. The off-center gold circle preserves the original warmth and gentle happiness.

Tradeoff: The niche strengthens the prayer association, though the abstract interior can also suggest a sheltered person or a place of wellbeing.

# 08 · ياسمين — Jasmine

**Idea:** A front-view Tunisian jasmine blossom on the brand teal: five pointed petals with a slight pinwheel twist. A small gold crescent sits in the centre, inside a teal ring. All five petals are one petal shape rotated by 72°.

**Symbolism:** Jasmine («الياسمين», the «مشموم» bouquet worn behind the ear) is the flower people most link with Tunisia, so the icon says "Tunisian" without a flag or a monument. The five petals are the five daily prayers, arranged as a cycle around one centre. The clockwise twist suggests time moving through the day. The gold crescent in the heart of the flower is the prayer cue: the lunar calendar and the call to prayer at the centre of Tunisian daily life. It opens to the upper right. Each petal has a deeper tone on its leading half, so the petal appears to turn, as in a real blossom.

**Palette:** #004D40 background edge and #00695C background centre (a subtle radial gradient, brand teal); #FFF8F0 petal ivory (brand cream); #E6D6B2 petal shade on the twisted half (1.36:1 against the ivory, so the twist still reads at 64px); #D4AF37 crescent gold (brand gold, 3.15:1 on #00695C). There are 3 foreground colours.

**Why it beats the current icon:** It is specific to Tunisia, not a generic Ottoman dome with pencil minarets. The motif is bold: it spans about 57dp (x 26-83, y 25-80), with 0% ink outside the 66dp safe circle. The old motif was small and thin. Every shape renders: the old crescent had zero area, while this one is a real filled crescent (outer r 6dp, minus an r 4.8dp disc offset 2.4dp, so it is 3.6dp at its thickest and about 12dp across). It has a real monochrome layer. It also stands out on a home screen full of mosque, crescent and Kaaba icons from other prayer apps, and the crescent still marks it as a prayer app.

**Small sizes & themed icon:** At 48px the five petals stay separate. The gap at the petal bases is 3.8dp, and it widens to about 19dp at the tips. The gold crescent reads as a crescent: the petals' inner arc grew from r 7.42 to r 8.5dp, so a teal ring at least 1.95dp wide separates the crescent from the petals. At 24px it still reads as a white five-petal flower with a gold mark in the centre, but the crescent shape is no longer distinct. The petal shade turns into a soft two-tone and does not become noise. In monochrome, the five petals are solid shapes around a round hole, and the crescent is a solid shape inside that hole. The gap around the crescent replaces the teal ring, so the crescent is cut out of the flower by negative space. The asymmetric pointed petals carry the twist without colour, and the crescent stays visible in the themed icon at 48px.

**Risks:** The prayer-times purpose rests on one small crescent, and the "5 petals = 5 prayers" reading has to be learned. A white five-petal flower can still look like a spa or wellness app at a glance, though the crescent and the slim twisted petals now push it away from frangipani. The jasmine is also linked to the 2011 "Jasmine Revolution", which some users may read politically. That is a content question for the owner to settle before this direction goes further. The horn tips of the crescent are sharp and soften to a blur below 36px. A mechmoum-style cluster (one flower with 2-3 buds) would be more specifically Tunisian, but it would shrink the main flower and the crescent below the 48px limit, so this version keeps the single blossom.

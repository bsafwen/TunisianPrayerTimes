# 01 Volume Rug «سجادة الصمت»

**Idea.** A prayer rug seen from above is also the phone's volume slider turned all the way down: the rug is the track, and the white thumb rests at its foot showing a muted speaker.

**Silence.** A big speaker with a small × in a white disc, the same shape the Android volume panel (Pixel, Xiaomi, OnePlus) shows when muted. The × stays inside the disc and never touches the rug.

**Prayer.** Madder-red rug, cream inner border, sand fringe at both ends, one small Kairouan lozenge in the empty upper track. No niche, crescent or building.

**Palette.** Background #F7F3EA to #ECE3D3. Rug #BC4631 to #93301F. Border and lozenge #F3E2C0. Fringe #D2AE7A. Thumb #FFFFFF with a hard shadow #6E2216. Speaker and × #7F2A1C.

**Small sizes.** At 48px the rug, fringe, lozenge, thumb and speaker with × all read. At 24px it is a red rug with a white dot at its foot; the fringe becomes a soft edge. Themed icon: rug and fringe solid, thumb a hole, speaker and × as islands; clean in light and dark.

**Variants (_var).** b-nowaves: a speaker without waves says "speaker", not clearly "muted". c-badge: the thumb as a corner badge pushes the rug off-centre and shrinks the glyph. d-cross-plain: without the lozenge the rug looks like a phone or card. e-teeth: a kilim sawtooth under the head border is nice at 96px but noise at 48px. a-cross (final) is the clearest.

**Risks.** The rounded rug may look like a card or ticket to some. The fringe may read as a comb or chip pins. The slider idea clicks best for people who know the Android volume panel.

# 01 Volume Rug «سجادة الصمت»

**Idea.** A woven prayer rug that is also the phone's volume slider at zero. A cream track runs down from the lozenge; the white thumb rests at the foot with a muted speaker.

**Silence.** The Android volume-panel muted speaker. The × is about 20% larger and nearly as heavy as the speaker, so it holds at 48px and still shows at 36px. It stays inside the white disc and never touches the rug.

**Prayer.** Madder-red rug, one cream border, a Kairouan lozenge at the head, undyed-wool fringe at both ends. No niche, crescent or building.

**Palette.** Ground #EBDDC3 to #DBC9A8. Rug #B5402C to #A93A27 (6%). Border, lozenge, track #F3E2C0. Fringe #FFFBF2. Thumb #FFFFFF. Glyph #8A2E1F.

**Small sizes and themed icon.** At 48px every part reads. At 24px it is a red rug with a white dot at its foot. Themed: solid rug, cream parts and thumb cut out, glyph as an island.

**This revision.** Corners cut from 8.5 to 4.5dp. One border instead of two. Body 38 x 46dp (it was 36 x 52). Six uneven wool strands, 6 to 7dp long, splaying at the tips. No shadow. A drawn track. A deeper ground so the white fringe shows.

Not applied: no border (the lead asked for one; see _var/g-bands). 1.2dp strands (they vanish at 48px). Thumb off the rug (_var/j-foot: the two strands left read as legs). Height of 52dp or less (the fringe needs the length; all ink stays in the 66dp circle).

**Risks.** The themed icon can still look like a chip. Lozenge, track and disc may read as a pendulum. People who don't know the volume panel see "mute" and "rug" but not the slider.

# 07 · سجادة صامتة · Muted Rug

**Idea.** A blue Kairouan prayer rug with a round "muted" badge on its corner, like the mute badge on a video-call tile.

**Silence.** A deep-navy badge holds a white speaker with a bold round-capped ×, the mute sign from calls. A 2.8dp ring of ground colour keeps the badge off the rug, so the × touches only the speaker.

**Prayer.** Fringed ends, one white border and one bold lozenge. No niche or arch, so it is neither a door nor a headstone.

**Palette.** Rug `#1F5FA8`, border and lozenge `#FFFFFF`, badge `#16365F` with a `#FFFFFF` glyph, whitewash ground `#FBF9F5` to `#F0EAE0`.

**Small sizes and themed icon.** Clean at 48px; at 24px the × still shows. Themed: rug and badge are two islands with their details cut out.

**This revision.** Sawtooth and inner dot removed. 6 round-ended tassels and a 2dp corner radius match the round badge. The badge moved to the bottom-right, out of the corner where One UI and Pixel draw notification dots. Calm navy instead of alert red. The × went from a thin mark to a 3dp stroke. Group re-centred: ink centroid (53.5, 53.4), was (52.8, 53.8). `_var/teal-cream` (cream rug on `#00695C`, red badge) got the same changes.

**Crescent badge.** `r2-crescent` reads as a moon at every size. Next to a rug it says "night" or "Ramadan" more than "muted", so the speaker × stays.

**Ignored with reason.** A badge fully detached from the rug (`r2-apart`) only fits the safe circle if the rug shrinks back toward a phone shape. The badge also goes tiny at 48px.

**Risks.** Read literally, the metaphor says "the rug is muted". The owner should approve it. The navy badge is quieter than red, so it is easier to miss on a busy home screen.

Privacy Policy — Mosque TV

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Tunisian Prayer Times for mosque TVs («أوقات الصلاة تونس», "the TV app") shows the prayer times, the iqamah and the adhkar on a mosque's screen. It is developed by Safwen Baroudi and comes in two builds: the Google Play build (com.tunisianprayertimes.tv) and the GitHub build for boxes without Google Play (com.tunisianprayertimes.tv.github). This policy covers both. The phone app has its own privacy policy.

1. No accounts, no analytics

The TV app has no accounts and no sign-in. It uses no analytics, no crash-reporting service and no advertising, and it sends no device identifier anywhere. The developer receives no data from it.

2. What stays on the TV

Prayer times are computed on the device. Everything the mosque sets is stored on the TV only: the mosque's name and delegation, the iqamah times and prayer durations, Ramadan and Eid dates, display options, the adhkar lists, written announcements, and the images copied from a USB key or sent from a phone. The TV also keeps a local log of its own events (start-ups, crashes, sleep) and the confirmation of its clock, shown on its maintenance page.

None of this leaves the TV. The app's data is excluded from Android's backup and from transfers to a new device. The settings are erased by Settings → «متقدم» → «إعادة ضبط الشاشة», the images by «حذف الصور وملفات الإعلانات», and all of the app's data by clearing it in the device's settings or uninstalling the app.

3. Network requests

The TV app works without internet. When the TV is online, it makes these requests, over HTTPS:

  • Weather (on by default; can be turned off in the settings or from the phone): about every half hour, a request to api.open-meteo.com with the latitude and longitude of the delegation chosen for the prayer times. These are the delegation's reference point, rounded to three decimals, not the position of the device: the app has no location permission. Open-Meteo's own terms and privacy policy apply to that request.
  • Time check: when the system has no network time to give the app (always on Android 12 and older), a HEAD request to api.open-meteo.com, of which only the date of the answer is read.
  • Official Ramadan and Eid dates: around the start of Ramadan and the two Eids, small JSON files read from this project's GitHub Pages site (bsafwen.github.io).
  • Updates (GitHub build only): about once a day, the list of this project's releases from api.github.com, with the app's name and version; a new version is then downloaded from github.com. The Google Play build is updated by Google Play.

As with any internet request, these servers see the TV's IP address. No personal data, settings or identifier is sent with the requests.

4. The phone dashboard (local network)

An administrator can manage the TV from a phone or computer on the same local network. The dashboard is served by the TV itself, only while an administrator has started a session on the TV, and only to whoever scans the code the TV shows for that session. A session ends after a quarter of an hour without use, and after two hours in any case. The dashboard never goes through the internet; images and settings sent from the phone are stored on the TV only.

5. USB keys

The TV reads a USB key only in the app's own folder on it (Android/data/<package>/files): the settings file mosque-tv.json, images, and written announcements. It writes the current settings file there. It needs no storage permission and reads nothing else on the key.

6. Permissions

  • Internet and network state — for the optional requests of Section 3, and the local phone dashboard.
  • Run at start-up, keep awake, display over other apps — so the mosque's screen comes back by itself after a power cut and stays on.
  • Device administrator — used only when an installer sets the box up as a dedicated device (device owner). The app then keeps itself on screen (lock task), sets the box's time zone to Tunisia's (turning off the automatic time zone) and, when the box's automatic time is off, sets its clock to the confirmed time. No other device policy is used.
  • GitHub build only: installing its own updates; and "quick start", an accessibility service that brings the prayer screen back to the front after start-up or after the box's home screen appears. It only learns which app comes to the front: it does not read the screen's content, keys or gestures, and collects no data. Its permission to change secure settings is granted by a technician with adb, to turn the service on and to turn off Fire TV's sleep timer.

7. Children's privacy

The TV app collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app changes. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

تطبيق شاشة المسجد لا حساب فيه ولا إحصاءات ولا إعلانات، ولا يرسل أي معرّف للجهاز. كل الإعدادات والصور وسجلّ أحداث الشاشة تبقى على الجهاز، ولا تدخل في النسخ الاحتياطي لأندرويد. تُحسب أوقات الصلاة على الجهاز دون إنترنت.

إن اتصلت الشاشة بالإنترنت: تطلب الطقس من Open-Meteo بإحداثيات المعتمدية المختارة (لا موقع الجهاز، ويمكن إيقاف الطقس)، وتتحقق من الوقت بطلب إلى الخادم نفسه حين لا يعطيها النظام وقت الشبكة (ودائمًا على أندرويد 12 وما قبله)، وتقرأ تواريخ رمضان والعيد الرسمية من صفحة المشروع على GitHub، ونسخة GitHub وحدها تبحث فيه عن التحديثات. لوحة الإدارة من الهاتف تعمل على شبكة المسجد وحدها، بالرمز الذي تعرضه الشاشة لكل جلسة. ومفتاح USB يُقرأ في مجلد التطبيق وحده.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 17, 2026

Tunisian Prayer Times ("the App") is developed by Safwen Baroudi. This privacy policy explains how the App handles your information.

1. Information We Collect

The App collects and stores only the settings you configure, including:

  • Your selected municipality (delegation) in Tunisia
  • Your prayer silence preferences (duration, delay, mode)
  • Whether the silence feature is enabled or disabled

All of this data is stored locally on your device only and is never transmitted to any server.

If you use the GPS auto-detect feature, your device location is read momentarily to find the nearest municipality. The coordinates themselves are never stored or transmitted — only the resulting municipality selection is saved locally.

The App also uses Google Firebase Analytics to collect limited, privacy-safe usage and reliability events. These events help us understand whether important features are working and which parts of the App need improvement. Examples include which main tab was opened, whether a permission step succeeded, whether automatic silence was enabled, bucketed alarm counts or durations, Qibla calculation success or failure, and alarm scheduling health.

Analytics events are intentionally coarse. They do not include precise prayer timestamps, GPS coordinates, selected municipality name or ID, phone call details, contact information, ringtone names or file paths, or custom user-entered content. Advertising ID collection and ad personalization signals are disabled.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not collect, store, or transmit:

  • Personal identification (name, email, phone number)
  • Device identifiers (IMEI, Android ID, advertising ID)
  • GPS coordinates or precise location data
  • Phone call details (caller ID, call logs, call content)
  • Contacts, photos, or files
  • Crash reports or diagnostics
  • Advertising identifiers or data for ad personalization
  • Precise location, municipality name, or municipality ID in analytics

3. Network Access

All prayer time data is bundled within the App and no internet connection is required for the core silencing functionality.

The App makes one optional network request: once per day during Ramadan it fetches a small JSON file from GitHub to check whether this project has published a Ramadan or Aid prayer time adjustment based on public source data. No personal data is sent in this request. If the request fails or the device has no internet access, the App continues to work normally using the bundled data.

Firebase Analytics may send the limited events described in Section 1 to Google so we can review aggregated usage and reliability information. The App does not use analytics for advertising, profiling, or personalized marketing.

4. Permissions

The App requests the following permissions, each required for core functionality:

  • Do Not Disturb access — to silence your device during prayer times
  • Exact alarms — to schedule prayer time events precisely
  • Run after reboot — to reschedule alarms when your device restarts
  • Battery optimization exemption — to prevent the system from disabling scheduled alarms
  • Location (optional) — used only when you tap the GPS auto-detect button to find your nearest municipality. The App never accesses location in the background.
  • Internet access — for the optional Ramadan prayer adjustment check described in Section 3.
  • Vibration — to vibrate the phone at the end of a silence period when an incoming call was missed during silence.
  • Phone state — required to detect whether an incoming call was received while the phone was silenced, so the App can alert you with a vibration when silence ends. Only the RINGING state is observed; no call details (number, identity, duration) are read, stored, or transmitted.
  • Full-screen intent (Android 14+) — to display the wake alarm on the lock screen. The App prompts you to grant this permission only when you enable a wake alarm.
  • Notifications (Android 13+) — to show wake alarm notifications. No marketing or promotional notifications are ever sent.
  • Motion sensors (accelerometer / gyroscope) — used solely for the optional gyroscope maze wake-up challenge game. Sensor readings are processed in real time to move the on-screen ball and are never recorded, stored, or transmitted.

5. Third-Party Services

The App uses Google Firebase Analytics for the limited privacy-safe analytics described above. Firebase Analytics is not used for advertising, ad targeting, or ad personalization in this App.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell user data. Limited analytics events are processed by Google Firebase Analytics as described in this policy. Local App settings remain on your device and are not transmitted to us.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

All settings are stored locally on your device. You can delete all data at any time by clearing the App's data in your device settings or by uninstalling the App.

8. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

# 03 · Ring of Sad («حلقة الصمت»)

**Idea.** ص, the first letter of صلاة (prayer) and صمت (silence), written as one pen stroke that becomes ⊖, the Android Do Not Disturb sign.

**Silence.** On a phone, a ring with a bar across it means "Do Not Disturb". Here it is drawn as calligraphy. The ring stays open by about 45° on the upper left, so it never reads as a no-entry sign. It is never red.

**Prayer.** The bar is the long, flat Maghrebi loop of ص, with a 3.2dp slit inside it and a clear tooth at its left end. From the tooth the pen turns over a round shoulder and sweeps into the ring. The ring is heaviest at the lower right and thins to a point at the tail.

**Palette.** Navy ink `#1C2A47` on limewash paper, a gradient from `#FBF7EF` to `#EFE6D4`.

**Small sizes.** At 48px the open ring, the bar and the tooth all read. At 24px the ⊖ takes over: the gap stays open and the bar reads as one bar. The themed icon is the same stroke in one colour, with the slit cut as a hole.

**Variants (`_var/`).**
- `navy`: less thick-thin contrast. It looks closer to a plain pipe.
- `contrast`: more contrast. More like a brush, but too thin at 24px.
- `gold`: a gold tooth. It breaks the single stroke, so it was rejected. A gold dot would turn ص into ض.
- `sbs`: Sidi Bou Said blue `#1A4F8E`. Very Tunisian, a good alternative, but less contrast when small.

The final sits between `navy` and `contrast`.

**Risks.** Because the bar joins the ring, some people may first see a rotated "e" or a spiral. Anyone who has never used Do Not Disturb will miss the silence link.

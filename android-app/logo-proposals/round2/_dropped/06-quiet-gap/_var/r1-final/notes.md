# 06 «فسحة الهدوء» The Quiet Gap

**Idea.** A voice-note waveform goes quiet in the middle, and an upright prayer rug stands in that quiet slot: sound all day, silence at prayer time, then sound again.

**How silence reads.** On each side the bars go short, tall, short as they near the rug, then leave an empty margin. The rug stands where the loudest sound would be. No speaker, no slash.

**How prayer reads.** A madder-red rug with a pointed mihrab arch, drawn as an open cream band, and four tassels at each end.

**Palette.** Sand background `#F6EFE3`, Sidi Bou Said blue bars `#1F4E8C`, madder rug and fringe `#B5412F`. The arch is `#F6EFE3` (a cut-through, as in the themed icon).

**Small sizes.** At 48px the waveform, the red rug and its pointed arch all read. The tassels blur into a soft fringe edge. At 24px it is a blue waveform with a red block in the middle; the arch is gone. Themed icon: one colour, arch cut out, clean in light and dark.

**Variants** (in `_var/`):
- Two bars per side looked like a dumbbell.
- A ramp envelope looked like a level meter.
- Terracotta bars with a blue rug had less contrast.
- Dots in the gap looked like a chain.
- Tan fringe vanished at 48px.
- A round closed arch or a solid niche looked like a battery.
- A gradient background looked muddier.

The final has three uneven 5dp bars per side, a pointed open arch and a taller rug.

**Risks.** The 66dp circle limits the width, so the gap is only about 4dp on each side. A waveform alone says "audio": the rug carries the prayer meaning. Some may first read the fringed rug as a microchip. The mark is wide and light (19% ink).

# 08 «ششش» Shhh (revision 2)

**Idea.** One bold sheen (ش), the letter Tunisians type as «ششش» for "shh", drawn as a sound dying away.

**Silence.** The three teeth step down hard in reading order (100%, 74%, 47% of the tallest). The kashida runs far to the left, thinning to a soft round tip (3.7dp) and fading from cream toward the ground colour.

**Prayer.** No symbol (option a). Beside the dots a crescent is a bedtime moon (`r2-c-tucked`), horns-up on the left a smile (`r2-c-finial-left`), on top of the dots a fleck at 48px (`r2-c-crown`). A rug at the kashida's end (`r2-b-rug`) reads as a tassel and shortens the trail. Prayer comes only from the app name.

**Palette.** Cream `#FFF3E2` on Nabeul terracotta `#CC6A3E` to `#A0462A`. The kashida fades to a 60% mix with the ground at its tip.

**Small sizes, themed icon.** At 48px the step-down and the fading tail read clearly. At 24px the tooth gaps stay open and the tail almost disappears, as intended. The themed icon is flat, so the tail only thins there.

**Changes.** Hard step-down, longer fading kashida, round dots matching the round tooth tops and tip, crescent dropped, letter 4% larger. Ink: 10.3% to 13.7%.

**Critique not applied.** Ghost sheens (`r2-echo`) look like motion blur: vibration, not quiet. Kashida dashes (`r2-dashes`) look like extra letter dots and vanish at 24px. The ink centroid (59, 58) stays right of centre because of the long faint tail; centring it would shorten the tail. In the masks it looks balanced.

**Risks.** The pun works only for Arabic readers. Without the crescent it reads even more like a brand monogram (café, pottery). Ask a few Tunisian users "what does this app do?"; if fewer than half say silence, keep it for the splash screen or wordmark.
